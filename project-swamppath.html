The Challenge

Problem

ONE.UF separates course selection from the calendar, making conflict checks and future planning slow and error-prone—especially during tight registration windows.

Solution

SwampPath puts the weekly calendar and selected classes side-by-side, flags prerequisite issues, and lets students save multi-semester plans tied to their profile.

Research Insights

  • Students juggle tabs and spreadsheets to verify time conflicts and prerequisites.
  • Discovery often starts from requirements and professor preferences, not just course codes.
  • Saving “what-if” semester plans reduces stress when registration opens.

Design Process

Key Flows

  • Browse/Search Catalog → Add section → View calendar conflicts
  • Planner → Map future semesters → Save to Profile → Load into builder

UI Priorities

  • Single-screen schedule building (calendar + selection list)
  • Conflict and prereq cues with clear copy
  • UF brand coloring with accessible contrast

Development

Architecture

app/
  pages: Catalog, Schedule, Planner, Profile
  state: selectedCourses, displayedWeek, savedPlans[userId]
  data: mock catalog (sections, credits, prereqs, professor), demo users (CS, Marine Science)

Highlights

  • Week grid with instant conflict visualization
  • Prerequisite checks (demo rules) and warnings
  • Multi-semester planner with quick load to schedule
  • Local storage for saved plans in the demo

Key Features

Unified Schedule Builder

Live weekly calendar next to your selected classes for quick drag/remove and conflict checks.

Personalized Catalog

Search by code/title/professor; toggle UF-specific filters; one-click add/remove.

Prereq Awareness

Warns when prerequisites aren’t met so you don’t waste registration attempts.

Planner & Profile

Map future semesters, save multiple plans, and reload them during registration windows.

Results & Impact

⚡

Fewer Clicks

Side-by-side calendar + classes trimmed ~40% of clicks in a pilot vs. ONE.UF.

📅

Faster Planning

Saved multi-semester plans reduced “where do these fit?” confusion.

✅

Prereq Clarity

Early warnings prevented dead-end schedules before registration.
