Vendor Label Validation Tool

Publix Technology · Summer 2025 Software Engineer Intern

One-liner Xamarin.Forms tool that validates seafood labels via GS1 scanning—cutting processing time from 15 minutes to ~15 seconds.
Role UX design, front-end (C#/.NET), validation logic, Xamarin.Forms UI
Timeline 10 weeks

Tools & Stack

C#/.NETXamarin.FormsMVVM SQLTelerik UIGS1 Parser Visual StudioAzure DevOps

Problem

Before: associates manually validated labels by checking GTINs, vendor info, weight, and harvest data up to 15 minutes per label and error-prone.

Solution

Mobile barcode scanning parses GS1 DataMatrix and validates label metadata instantly, improving accuracy and compliance.

Goals & Hypothesis

H1: Automating validation through scanning reduces time by >90% and improves accuracy.

< 20s Target validation time
100% Scan accuracy for supported GS1 barcodes
Positive Feedback from field testers

Research

  • Interviews with internal team and seafood QA specialists
  • GS1 AI documentation for parsing and decoding
  • Review of existing validation workflows
Common rejection reasons: incorrect weights, missing pack dates, and vendor details—automation reduces rework.

Persona / JTBD

Publix QA Associate — “When I receive seafood shipments, I want to quickly validate the vendor label so I can approve it without manual entry or errors.”

Information Architecture

  • Home → Vendor Label Validation
  • Scan Barcode → Display Label Info → Warnings/Errors
  • Past Scans List → Detail

Design

High-Fidelity

  • Telerik RadDataForm for clean, grouped fields
  • Fields: GTIN, Vendor, Stewardship, Harvest Method, Weight, Pack Date, State of Origin
  • Invalid/missing fields highlighted clearly

Development Highlights

GS1 Parsing

Application Identifiers: 01 (GTIN), 10 (Lot), 310x (Net Weight), 11/13 (Dates). Mapped to the domain model and validated.

Custom Validation

  • Presence of required fields
  • State of origin rules (USA/RTC only)
  • Quantity vs. Weight (at least one required)

Display & Binding

  • Image bound by SKU (not surfaced directly)
  • Weight conversion (e.g., 001550 → 15.50 lbs)
  • Pack date formatted dd/MM/yy
  • Graceful fallbacks on enums/nulls

Results

  • Validation time reduced from ~15 minutes to ~15 seconds
  • Real-time scan results and history
  • Android enterprise devices supported
  • Higher trust via clear validation indicators and raw fallback

Lessons

  • Design for imperfect data; fallbacks matter
  • Real-world GS1 data requires broad testing
  • Accessibility (contrast, size, spacing) is critical in field apps
