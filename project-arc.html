The challenge

First responders face intense trauma daily, yet support access is limited by stigma, awareness, and fragmented systems.

Our solution ARC provides post-call check-ins, curated resources, and confidential connections to therapists or peer mentors.

Research insights

Surveyed 41 first responders to understand habits, comfort, and needs.

61%
Aware of resources, not using
40%
Stigma / fear of judgment
78%
Rated concept useful (≥3/5)
Top requests: peer mentor chat & therapist scheduling. Pillars: confidentiality, speed, trust.

Design process

  • Flows for onboarding, post-call check-ins, and resources
  • Low-fi wires → high-fi prototype (Poppins, navy/orange)
  • A11y: strong contrast, large touch targets
  • Heuristic reviews for tone & flow

Wireframes, component library, and interaction notes for crisis contexts.

Key features

  • Confidential contacts: therapists, mentors, trusted
  • AI-powered severity cues & guided conversation
  • Curated resource hub (articles, videos)
  • Anonymous usage paths to reduce stigma

What’s next
