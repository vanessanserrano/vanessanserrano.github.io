UF Events — Campus Events Hub

A centralized, student-first way to discover and plan campus events across clubs, colleges, and departments.

HCI course project (research → prototype)
Vanessa Serrano · Frederick Blanco · Andres Cortes
Figma (research, wires, hi-fi prototype)

Interactive prototype

Open in Figma →

Overview

Students—especially newcomers—struggle to find timely, accurate event information scattered across Instagram, flyers, club sites, and email blasts. UF Events consolidates everything into one place with powerful filtering, a searchable catalog, favorites/RSVP, and calendar planning to boost awareness and attendance.

Research insights

  • Students miss events due to fragmented sources and last-minute discovery.
  • Instagram is common but unreliable for full coverage; official pages are inconsistent; email is noisy.
  • Personalization matters: interests/major-based recommendations and reminders without inbox overload.
Design pillars Fast discovery · Clear filters · Trustworthy details · Light-touch reminders.

Personas

Jasmine — Organized planner

  • Needs centralized, scheduled info; prefers calendar and week views.
  • Wants integration with existing tools and interest-based suggestions.

Sal — Exploring campus

  • Wants an easy way to find weekend events and meet people.
  • Needs clear event details and simple reminders.

Flows & wireframes

Key flows

  • Discover → Filter by category/date → View details → Save/RSVP
  • Search by keyword → Open event → Add to calendar → Reminder

Solution

  • Unified listing of campus events with clear categories and quick filters.
  • Interest-based recommendations, search, and favorites/RSVP.
  • Calendar preview, add-to-calendar, and light reminders (push/OS calendar).
  • Shareable event cards for socials or group chats.

Outcomes & next

  • Delivered high-fidelity prototype for evaluation and usability feedback.
  • Next: connect official feeds, refine recommendations, pilot with first-year cohorts.
