UF Events — Campus events hub

A centralized, student-first way to discover and plan campus events.

Scope HCI course project (research → prototype)
Team Vanessa Serrano, Frederick Blanco, Andres Cortes
Tools Figma (research, wireframes, hi-fi prototype)

Prototype

Open in Figma

Overview

Freshmen and newly involved students struggle to find accurate, timely event information scattered across Instagram, websites, posters, and Discord. UF Events proposes a single destination with powerful filters, a searchable catalog, favorites/RSVP, and calendar planning to boost awareness and attendance.

Research insights

  • Students miss events due to fragmented information and last-minute discovery.
  • Instagram is common but unreliable for full coverage; emails are noisy; official pages can be hard to navigate.
  • Students want recommendations by interests/major and reminders without inbox overload.

Personas

Jasmine — Organized planner

  • Needs centralized, scheduled information; prefers calendar-style views.
  • Wants integration with existing tools and interest-based recommendations.

Sal — Exploring campus

  • Wants an easy way to find weekend events and meet people.
  • Needs reminders and simple event details.

Solution

  • Unified listing of campus events with clear categories and filters.
  • Search, interest-based recommendations, and favorites/RSVP.
  • Calendar preview with reminders and simple sharing.

Outcomes & next

  • Delivered high-fidelity prototype for evaluation and usability feedback.
  • Next: connect to official feeds, refine recommendations, pilot with first-year cohorts.
