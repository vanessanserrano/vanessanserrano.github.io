Eclipse — Gaming Device Marketing Site

A marketing website for Eclipse, a hybrid gaming device concept combining Nintendo Switch portability with Steam Deck power. Class project showcasing brand identity, product positioning, and responsive web design.

The challenge

Create a cohesive marketing presence for a fictional gaming device that bridges the gap between portable and high-performance gaming.

Our approach While teammates focused on 3D rendering the device hardware, I designed the complete marketing website with brand identity, product showcase, feature highlights, and responsive layouts.

Team collaboration

My Role: Web Design

  • Brand identity and color system (dark purple, orange, black)
  • Complete marketing website design in Figma
  • Product page layouts and feature sections
  • Responsive design for mobile and desktop

Team Members: 3D Rendering

  • Created 3D model of Eclipse device
  • Rendered product shots for marketing materials
  • Designed device interface and controls
  • Collaborated on overall product concept

Design process

  • Research: Analyzed Nintendo Switch and Steam Deck marketing to identify positioning gaps
  • Brand Identity: Developed dark purple/orange/black palette to convey premium gaming aesthetics
  • Information Architecture: Structured site with hero, features, specs, and CTA sections
  • Visual Design: Created high-contrast layouts emphasizing product renders and key features
  • Prototyping: Built interactive Figma prototype with navigation and hover states

Site features

  • Hero Section: Bold product introduction with 3D device render and primary CTA
  • Feature Highlights: Modular sections showcasing portability, performance, and battery life
  • Product Specifications: Technical details presented in accessible, scannable format
  • Responsive Design: Optimized layouts for mobile, tablet, and desktop viewports
  • Brand Consistency: Cohesive color system, typography, and component library throughout

Cross-Discipline Collab

Successfully delivered a cohesive marketing presence that complemented the team's 3D work. Gained experience in brand identity design, product marketing, and cross-functional collaboration with 3D artists.

Reflection

  • Brand identity work requires balancing boldness with usability—too much contrast can hurt readability
  • Marketing sites need clear hierarchy and CTAs to guide user journey effectively
  • Collaborating with 3D artists taught me to design around high-quality assets and showcase them properly
  • Responsive design considerations should start in wireframing, not as an afterthought
